# Emo avatar pack

Emo is a bundled sample avatar pack for Explainer Studio. It follows the v1 avatar pack format, with full-body pose sheets and separate eye and mouth sprite sheets.

Emo is a chibi screen robot with a compact body, consistent graphite and silver construction, and a flat 2D cartoon finish with clean outlines and simple cel shading. Cyan LED eyes and mouth sprites stay inside its black screen. Talking eyes use solid cyan shapes without inner dots. Horizontal gaze uses level eyes with a slight size difference toward the pointing direction. Diagonal gaze also offsets their height.

[View the composed pack preview](preview.png).

## Contents

- `poses/talking.png`: idle, present, like, thinking, idea, and celebrate.
- `poses/pointing.png`: seven directional pointing poses, including viewer-facing.
- `poses/emotion.png`: smile, grin, laugh, sad, worried, surprised, and angry.
- `eyes/talking.png` and `eyes/emotion.png`: gaze, blink, and matching expressions.
- `mouth/talking.png` and `mouth/emotion.png`: lip-sync and matching expression shapes.

## Use

Load this folder as an avatar pack in Explainer Studio. The manifest contains frame bounds and face placement. The preview shows the pack through the Studio compositor.
